KEY QUESTIONS TO PREPARE
════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What is Reinforcement Learning?
A: Agent learns by interacting with
   environment. Takes actions, gets
   rewards, learns to maximize total
   reward. No labeled data needed!

Q: What is MDP?
A: Markov Decision Process:
   S = State (inventory, days_left)
   A = Action (price level)
   R = Reward (revenue from sale)
   T = Transition (state change)

Q: What is Q-Learning?
A: Tabular RL algorithm.
   Q-table stores expected reward
   for every state-action pair.
   Q(s,a) ← Q(s,a) + α[r + γ·max Q(s',a') - Q(s,a)]
   Limited to small state spaces!

Q: What is DQN?
A: Deep Q-Network.
   Replaces Q-table with neural network.
   Two innovations:
   1. Experience replay (breaks correlations)
   2. Target network (stable training)
   Scales to any state space!

Q: What is PPO?
A: Proximal Policy Optimization.
   Policy-based (not value-based).
   Actor-Critic architecture.
   Clips updates: prevents large changes.
   Same algorithm used in ChatGPT!

Q: What is Actor-Critic?
A: Two networks:
   Actor  → WHAT action (probabilities)
   Critic → HOW GOOD is state (value)
   Work together for stable learning!

Q: What behaviors did PPO learn?
A: TWO key behaviors PROVED:
   1. Deadline Discounting
      Drops prices near departure
      to clear remaining inventory!
   2. Scarcity Premium Pricing
      Raises prices when inventory low
      (supply and demand effect!)

Q: What is imbalanced dataset?
A: IMPORTANT (learned from P1 review!)
   When one class has WAY more samples.
   NOT null values! Class distribution!
   In P2: Revenue varies widely across
   agents showing performance differences.

Q: What is experience replay?
A: DQN innovation.
   Store experiences in buffer.
   Sample RANDOM batches for training.
   Breaks temporal correlations!
   Makes training stable!

Q: What is epsilon greedy?
A: Exploration vs exploitation.
   ε = probability of random action.
   Start: ε=1.0 (100% explore)
   End  : ε=0.01 (99% exploit)
   Gradually shifts to learned policy!
