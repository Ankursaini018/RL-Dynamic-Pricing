NEW QUESTIONS TO PREPARE:
==========================

Q: What is PPO?
A: Proximal Policy Optimization.
   Policy-based RL algorithm by OpenAI.
   Uses Actor-Critic architecture.
   Clips policy updates for stability.
   Same algorithm used in ChatGPT RLHF!

Q: PPO vs DQN difference?
A: DQN → Value-based (learns Q-values)
         Off-policy (uses replay buffer)
   PPO → Policy-based (learns probabilities)
         On-policy (uses fresh rollouts)
         More stable training!

Q: What is Actor-Critic?
A: Two neural networks:
   Actor  → WHAT action to take
            (outputs probabilities)
   Critic → HOW GOOD is current state
            (outputs single value)
   Work together for better learning!

Q: What is clipping in PPO?
A: Prevents too-large policy updates.
   ratio = new_prob / old_prob
   clip(ratio, 0.8, 1.2) for ε=0.2
   Keeps new policy close to old one.
   Makes training very stable!

Q: What is GAE?
A: Generalized Advantage Estimation.
   Better way to compute advantages.
   A(s,a) = Q(s,a) - V(s)
   Balances bias and variance.
   Makes PPO more sample efficient!
